USING THE MEMBERS AREA
The sidebar selects a category. Open shows the full PNG preview.
PNG and PDF save individual files. The category toolbar offers a combined
category PDF. Individual activity PDFs are in files/activities.
The top download button offers the main collection, bonuses, or everything.
Search covers every activity. Done marks are stored in this browser only.

OWNER SETUP NOTE
This static HTML library does not authenticate purchasers or validate
DigitalGoat orders. For purchase-based access, place it behind your membership
platform or configure hosting access controls. Completion marks are not access
protection. This library includes all bonuses; provide only the main collection
to Essential purchasers unless you intentionally include extras.

USE AND PRINTING
Read the opening guidance before leading activities. Use comfortable ranges,
allow recovery, and adapt tasks for your singers. Print A4 at actual size or
fit to the available paper, including Letter. PDF text remains sharp.
For your own choir leadership, teaching, and internal rehearsals. Do not resell
or publicly redistribute the collection.
