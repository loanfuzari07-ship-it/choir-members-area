Make the library work for you

Choose one useful target

Open a category and read an activity's goal. Prepare its materials, model the three steps, and use the Look / Listen For box to notice the result. Most activities take three to eight minutes, including teaching and recovery.

Use the notes before you lead

The complete activity collection explains note-number patterns, rhythm words, comfortable pitch choices, and pacing. Download the main collection and read its opening pages first.

Open, save, and print

Open shows the full PNG preview. The PNG button saves that image; PDF saves the printable activity page. Each category also has a combined PDF containing its ten activities. In Bonuses, choose a collection to open its individual sheets; each has a separate PNG and PDF. The top download button offers the main collection, all bonuses, or everything together.

Keep progress personal

Done marks activities you have explored. It is a planning aid, not a test. Progress is stored only in this browser on this device and may be lost if browser storage is cleared. The files can be used again whenever useful.

Adapt with care

Use a comfortable range and volume, rest between attempts, and allow speech, listening, seated participation, or a shorter pattern. Stop a task that causes pain, dizziness, or strain. Do not ask a hoarse or tired singer to push through.
