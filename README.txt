CHOIR IN HARMONY - YOUR REHEARSAL LIBRARY

Open index.html to browse the members area. Keep the assets, files, and previews folders next to it. All resources can also be opened directly from those folders.

MAIN COLLECTION
120 different activities in 12 categories, with 10 activities in each category. Every activity has a complete illustrated PNG and a one-page printable PDF. Each category has a combined PDF, too.

BONUS COLLECTIONS
1. Ready-to-Use Vocal Routines: 20 individual routines.
2. Stage-Ready Choir: 12 guide chapters and one event planning sheet.
3. Rehearsals in Harmony: 30 individual rehearsal plans.
4. Express Warm-Ups: 40 individual warm-up sequences.

Open Bonuses, choose a collection, and open any sheet to see its complete image. Save its PNG or PDF, or download the complete bonus book.

DOWNLOAD EVERYTHING
The button at the top of the members area offers three choices: all 120 activities, all four bonus books, or everything together. The complete collection contains 250 pages, including covers, introductions, category pages, and planning sheets.

USE THE RESOURCES
Read the opening instructions in the main collection before leading the activities. Choose a useful goal, prepare the materials, model the task, and notice one result in your real music. The adaptation notes help you adjust each task to your group.

Done marks are a personal planning aid saved in the current browser on the current device. You can reuse every resource as often as it serves your rehearsals.

The batches folder contains twelve ZIP files, each with ten individual activity PDFs.

Use and print these materials for your own choir leadership, teaching, and internal rehearsals. Do not resell or publicly redistribute the collection.
